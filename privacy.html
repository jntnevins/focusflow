Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Focus Flow Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Focus Flow YouTube channels for uploading the owner's own videos to the owner's own channels. The only person who signs in to the app is that owner.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is connected, and
  • upload videos and thumbnails and set their titles, descriptions, tags, audience ("made for kids") setting, and publish time.

The app does not access comments, subscribers, viewer data, analytics, or any other user's data.

How data is stored

Google sign-in tokens and a log of uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. The app does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's token files from the owner's computer also removes all stored access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the Focus Flow YouTube channels.
